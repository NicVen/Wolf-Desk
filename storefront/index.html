What it does

Five reads, one number, zero guesswork.

Every instrument is scored on the same five things a desk checks before it commits. You see the number and exactly what's driving it — no black box.

🎯

The setup score

Catalyst, trend, position-in-range, supply/demand and volatility fit — rolled into one 0–100 grade. More reads lining up = a higher score.

🧭

Market regime

A mechanical Markov read of whether price is trending up (BULL), down (BEAR) or going sideways (SIDE) — and how sticky that state has been.

Real, or just noise?

A Deflated-Sharpe check asks if the edge in this direction is statistically real — deflated for how many instruments are tested, so a lucky chart doesn't fool you.
