How it is worked out

  • Risk = distance from entry to stop.
  • Reward = distance from entry to target.
  • Risk:reward = 1 : (reward ÷ risk).
  • Break-even win rate = 1 ÷ (1 + reward ÷ risk).

Worked example

Gold at 2,000, stop at 1,990, target at 2,030. Risk is 10, reward is 30, so the ratio is 1:3. You would need to win just over 25% of trades like this to break even. With $100 at risk, a win pays $300.

Quick table

  • 1:1 needs a 50% win rate
  • 1:1.5 needs 40%
  • 1:2 needs 33.3%
  • 1:3 needs 25%
