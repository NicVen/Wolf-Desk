Exact installs and uninstalls: Google counts those itself. Open Play Console, then STAALCALIBUR, then Statistics. This page counts what happens inside the app and on staalwag.com. "Phones with the app" means phones that opened the installed app at least once since counting started. "Probably uninstalled" means installed phones not opened for 30 days or more.

No personal details are stored. No names, emails, keys or IP addresses. Country comes from the phone's time zone setting. Each phone is a random code that can't be traced back to a person.
