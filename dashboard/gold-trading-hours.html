Gold's trading week

  • Opens: Sunday 6pm New York time.
  • Daily break: 5pm to 6pm New York time, Monday to Thursday. Most brokers show no prices in that hour.
  • Closes: Friday 5pm New York time.

The key times of a gold day

  • Asia (Shanghai 9am to 3:30pm Beijing time): China is one of the biggest gold markets. Asian hours are usually calmer.
  • London open (8am London): volume picks up as Europe starts.
  • London fix (10:30am and 3pm London): the twice-daily LBMA Gold Price.
  • COMEX open (8:20am New York): the main US gold futures session runs to 1:30pm New York.
  • US news (8:30am New York): jobs, inflation and other big US releases come out at this time and often move gold sharply.
  • London and New York overlap (8am to noon New York): usually the busiest part of the day.

Worked example: Auckland in November

Auckland is on summer time (UTC+13) in November, and New York is on UTC−5, so Auckland is 18 hours ahead. The COMEX open (8:20am New York) is 2:20am the next day in Auckland, the US news at 8:30am is 2:30am, and the daily break (5pm to 6pm New York) is 11am to noon. Gold opens for the week on Monday at noon Auckland time.
