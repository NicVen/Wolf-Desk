Privacy Policy — STAALCALIBUR

Last updated: 6 October 2026 · Operated by STAALWAG

This policy explains what the STAALCALIBUR app and the STAALWAG website (staalwag.com) collect, why, and your choices. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the service.

What we collect

  • Activation key & device identifier: when you unlock the app, your licence key and a device identifier are checked against our licensing service to confirm the key is valid and to prevent sharing.
  • Contact you provide: if you start a trial or buy, the email or messaging ID you enter, used only to deliver your key and account notices.
  • Basic usage/diagnostic data: non-identifying information such as app version and whether a check-in succeeded, used to keep the service running.
  • Guardian (optional add-on): only if you install the Guardian EA in your own MetaTrader 5, it sends your account balance, equity, open positions and the trades you ask about, so the app can warn you about risk. It is read-only: it cannot place, change or close trades.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your name, contacts, photos, location, or files.
  • We never have access to your money, and we cannot place, change or close trades for you.
  • We do not sell your data to anyone.

Payments

Purchases happen on our website through third-party payment providers (e.g. a crypto payment processor, PayPal, or a card processor). Those providers handle your payment details under their own privacy policies; we receive only confirmation that a payment succeeded and the licence it relates to. The app itself does not process payments.

How we use the data

Only to: verify your licence, deliver your activation key, send service notices (such as renewal or expiry), and keep the service secure and working.

Data sharing

We share data only with the service providers that make the product work (hosting, payment processing, and, if you opt in, messaging for notices). We do not share it for advertising.

Data retention

We keep licence and contact records for as long as your access is active and for a reasonable period afterwards for accounting and anti-abuse. You can ask us to delete your records (see Contact).

Your choices

You can stop using the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes it from your device.

Delete your data: go to staalwag.com/delete-account (also linked inside the app). With your key and contact it happens straight away; otherwise we do it within 30 days.

Children

STAALCALIBUR is for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

Not financial advice

STAALCALIBUR provides decision-support and educational information only. It is not financial, investment, or trading advice. Trading carries substantial risk of loss; you are responsible for your own decisions.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: staalwag@gmail.com.
