The formula

End balance = start × (1 + monthly return)months, plus each deposit grown from the month it went in. The return is earned on the growing balance, so the curve bends upward over time.

Worked example: $1,000 at 5% a month

After 12 months: $1,000 × 1.0512 = $1,795.86, a gain of 79.6%. Simple 5% of $1,000 each month would give only $600 of gain. After 24 months the compounded balance is $3,225.10.

Losses compound too

One 10% losing month needs an 11.1% gain just to get back to where you were. The drawdown recovery calculator shows the climb back from any loss, and the lot size calculator keeps each loss to a fixed slice of the account.
