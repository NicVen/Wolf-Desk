What is a lot in forex?

A lot is how you size a trade. One standard lot is 100,000 units of a currency. Here's what that means in plain English, with a free converter.

Pip values are for pairs quoted in US dollars (EURUSD, GBPUSD, AUDUSD, NZDUSD). Gold is 100 ounces per lot at most brokers. Education only, not financial advice.

The lot sizes

  • Standard lot (1.00): 100,000 units. About $10 per pip on EURUSD.
  • Mini lot (0.10): 10,000 units. About $1 per pip.
  • Micro lot (0.01): 1,000 units. About $0.10 per pip. Most brokers' smallest size.

Worked example: 0.10 lots of EURUSD

0.10 lots is 10,000 euros. EURUSD moves from 1.1000 to 1.1030, which is 30 pips. At $1 per pip, the trade has changed by $30, up or down depending on which way you traded. On 1.00 lot the same move is $300; on 0.01 lots it's $3.

Gold works differently

One lot of gold (XAUUSD) is usually 100 ounces. If gold moves $5, from $2,400 to $2,405, 1 lot changes by $500 and 0.01 lots by $5. That's why gold lot sizes are often much smaller than forex ones for the same risk.

The right lot size depends on your stop

A lot size on its own doesn't tell you your risk. The distance to your stop does. The lot size calculator works backwards from how much you're willing to lose, and the pip value calculator covers every pair.
