The formula

Gain needed = 1 ÷ (1 − drawdown) − 1. After a loss, every gain is earned on a smaller balance, so the way back is always steeper than the way down.

  • 10% down needs 11.1% back.
  • 20% down needs 25% back.
  • 30% down needs 42.9% back.
  • 50% down needs 100% back.

Worked example: a $10,000 account down 20%

The balance is now $8,000. To get back to $10,000 you need $2,000, which is 25% of $8,000. If you risk 1% per trade and your winners make 2R (2% of the balance), that's about 12 winning trades in a row, or more with losers in between. At 3% a month it takes about 7.5 months.

Smaller risk, shallower holes

The easiest drawdown to recover is the one that stays small. The lot size calculator keeps each loss to a fixed slice of your account, and the prop firm drawdown calculator shows how much room your challenge has left.
