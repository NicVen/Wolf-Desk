How it is worked out

  • Today's floor = balance at start of today − (account size × daily limit %).
  • Overall floor = account size − (account size × max drawdown %).
  • Room left = equity now − the higher of the two floors.
  • Losing trades left = room left ÷ (account size × risk per trade %), rounded down.

Worked example

$100,000 challenge, 5% daily and 10% max. You started today at $101,000, so today's floor is $96,000. The overall floor is $90,000. Your equity is $99,500, so you have $3,500 of room today. At 1% ($1,000) per trade that is 3 full losing trades before you would break the daily rule.

Why traders keep risk small

At 1% per trade, five losses in a row costs about 5% and ends the day. At 0.5% it costs about 2.5%, leaving room to recover. The lot size calculator turns your risk % into a lot size.
