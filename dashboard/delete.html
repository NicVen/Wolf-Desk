Enter your activation key and the email or Telegram ID you used when you got it. If they match, your data is deleted straight away.

What gets deleted

  • Your email or Telegram ID.
  • The phone and MT5 account your key was linked to.

What we keep

  • Payment records (amount and date, no contact details), because tax law requires them.
  • Ideas you posted on the public ideas board, which never showed your name, stay without any link to you.

Lost your key?

Fill in only your email or Telegram ID and send it. We delete your data by hand within 30 days. You can also email staalwag@gmail.com.
