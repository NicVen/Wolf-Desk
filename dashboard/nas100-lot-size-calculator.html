NAS100 lot size calculator

How many lots of the Nasdaq 100 (NAS100, US100, USTEC) keep your loss inside your limit. Works with any broker: you enter what one point is worth. Free, no sign-up.

A point = a 1.0 move in the index. Lot sizes are rounded down to your broker's 0.01 step. Education only, not financial advice.

The formula

Lots = risk in dollars ÷ (stop in points × $ per point for 1 lot).

Worked example: $10,000 account, 1% risk, 50-point stop

1% of $10,000 is $100. At a broker where 1 lot is $1 per point, a 50-point stop costs $50 per lot, so you can trade 2.00 lots. At a broker where 1 lot is $20 per point, the same stop costs $1,000 per lot, so the size is 0.10 lots. Same trade, same risk, very different lot sizes. That's why you need your broker's number.

How to find your broker's $ per point

In MT5, long-press or right-click NAS100 and open Specification. Multiply the contract size by the price move of 1.0 point: if the contract size is 1, one point on 1 lot is $1; if it is 10, it's $10; if it is 20, it's $20. If your account isn't in dollars, the broker converts it.
