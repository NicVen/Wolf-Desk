The four sessions

  • Sydney: about 7am to 4pm Sydney time. It opens the forex week on Sunday evening in New York.
  • Tokyo: 9am to 6pm Tokyo time. Japan has no daylight saving, so this one never moves.
  • London: 8am to 5pm London time. It's the biggest forex centre by volume.
  • New York: 8am to 5pm New York time. The forex week closes on Friday at 5pm New York.

Why the times move twice a year

London and New York change their clocks on different dates, and Sydney changes the opposite way to both. For a few weeks each spring and autumn the gaps between sessions shift by an hour. This clock works from each city's own time, so it stays right all year.

Worked example: Johannesburg in November

Johannesburg is UTC+2 with no daylight saving. In November London is on UTC+0 and New York on UTC−5, so London runs 10:00 to 19:00 and New York 15:00 to 00:00 in Johannesburg time. They overlap from 15:00 to 19:00.
