How pip value is worked out

Pip value = pip size × contract size × lots, then turned into US dollars if the pair is not quoted in dollars.

  • EURUSD, GBPUSD, AUDUSD, NZDUSD: 0.0001 × 100,000 = $10 per pip on 1.00 lot.
  • USDJPY: 0.01 × 100,000 = 1,000 yen per pip. Divide by the USDJPY price to get dollars.
  • USDCAD, USDCHF: 0.0001 × 100,000 = 10 CAD or CHF per pip. Divide by the price to get dollars.
  • Gold (XAUUSD): 100 ounces per lot, so $100 per $1 move and $1 per $0.01 move.
  • Silver (XAGUSD): 5,000 ounces per lot, so $50 per $0.01 move.

Worked example: USDJPY

You trade 0.50 lots of USDJPY and the price is 150.00. One pip is 1,000 yen × 0.50 = 500 yen. 500 ÷ 150 = $3.33 per pip. A 20-pip stop would cost about $66.67.
